# Education Content
Content Guide

## Content Areas
- Class
- Subjects
- Brief topical notes per subject
- 10 questions per topic

## Classes Covered
We will start grade 5 class for our MVP then later populate with other classes throughout the CBC Curriculum

## Subjects Covered (We will have at least subjects for our MVP)
- **Mathematics**
- **English**
- **Science**
- Kiswahili
- Social Studies
- Creative Arts
- Music
- Physical and Health Education

## Notes and Questions
Every subject will cover 10 topics and will encompass 10 questions per topic. The questions will include answers which can be viewed after the student has completed attempting answering the 10 questions per topic.

> The questions will be in JSON for easy copy and paste when building.
